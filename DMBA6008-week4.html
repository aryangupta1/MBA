01 / The decision

NPV in a lease-versus-buy decision

NPV allows cash flows occurring at different points in time to be compared on a present-value basis. For mutually exclusive financing or acquisition options, compare the present value of the cash outflows and choose the option with the lower present cost.

02 / Discounting

Why a later dollar counts for less

Future payments are discounted because a dollar paid later has a lower present value than a dollar paid today.

PV = CFt / (1 + r)^t
Term What it is
CFtCash flow in period t
rHurdle / discount rate
tNumber of periods from today
  • A cash flow occurring immediately (t=0) is not discounted
  • As either r or t increases, the present value of a future outflow falls
  • The discount/hurdle rate may reflect the cost of financing the competing alternatives
03 / Worked example

Lease versus buy, at a 10% hurdle rate

Software can either be bought for $10,000 immediately, or leased over five annual payments with the first paid immediately. Both give access for the same five-year period, so the comparison is driven by the timing and present value of the cash flows.

PVlease = 2,200 + 2,200/1.10 + 2,200/1.10² + 2,200/1.10³ + 2,200/1.10⁴ = $9,174

My notes describe the lease as “five annual payments of $2000”, but the calculation they set out uses payments of 2,200 — and only 2,200 produces the $9,174 recorded below. Both are reproduced here exactly as written rather than reconciled.

Option Present value of cash outflows
Buy$10,000
Lease$9,174
Economic advantage of leasing$826

Leasing creates an $826 advantage relative to buying in this example. Although nominal lease payments total $11,000, comparing $11,000 with $10,000 would be misleading, because most lease payments occur in the future.

04 / Takeaways

What this recap is for

  • Do not compare nominal cash totals when their timing differs — discount them to a common point in time first
  • The hurdle rate can materially affect lease-versus-buy conclusions, because it determines how heavily future payments are discounted
  • For alternatives delivering the same underlying benefit, compare their present economic cost, not just the upfront price or the total contractual payments
  • NPV converts financing and investment alternatives into a common value measure, supporting decisions about asset acquisition, financing and capital allocation
