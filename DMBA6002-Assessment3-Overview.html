possibleplausibleprobablepreferable

Citation cautions

Three things to get right

  • Don't name the lecturers. The Module 8/9 speakers aren't named in the transcripts. Cite lecture ideas as the module (e.g. DMBA6002 Module 8, 2026), and cite Schoemaker / Schwartz / Selin / Wack separately as external support — never claim they are your lecturers.
  • F.I.T. is the unit's own framework. Cite it as a Module 6 concept; don't invent an external author for it. The Futures Cone is correctly Voros (2003).
  • Verify every reference. Page ranges, DOIs and volumes in the template list were reconstructed from standard records — check each against the original, and trim to only what you cite (minimum 20).
